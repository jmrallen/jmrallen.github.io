Selected Projects

A Global 20-Year Extension of the Earth Observation Record Using Orthorectified KH-4 and KH-9 Imagery
In progress
An approach for automatically processing KEYHOLE stereo imagery into ortho-images and stereo elevation models. The workflow uses tools from the Ames Stereo Pipeline software library to automate processing, and is validated on 500 images covering roughly 100,000 km² and spanning 1967–1984.
Land Tenure and Management Modify the Effect of Armed Conflict on Forest Cover: Evidence from Central Asia
In progress
A study of how the impacts of conflict on the environment, specifically deforestation, differ across land tenure regimes. Funded by a grant from the International Development Research Centre (IDRC).
